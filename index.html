Leadership Is A Verb

South Brunswick Aerospace Club also known as SB Aerospace is a student-run aerospace club. We are a youth focussed 501(c)(3) nonprofit, fiscally sponsored by the Hack Foundation, dedicated to launching the future of aerospace in South Brunswick & beyond.

Our Mission

At the South Brunswick Aerospace Club, leadership isn’t a title—it’s a verb. We turn curiosity into capability by challenging students to lead through action:

  • Build: Design, fabricate, and test rockets and other flight hardware.
  • Compete: Represent South Brunswick on the national stage in challenges like American Rocketry Challenge,NASA TechRise and other Aerospace competitions.
  • Mentor: Share knowledge through peer-to-peer training and local youth workshops.
Our club motto is:

Ad astra per doctrinam aerospatii et terram firmam STEM

To the stars through aerospace education with a solid understanding of STEM
What we do

We do Rocket Science for fun!

We turn ideas into real projects—and give students room to build, test, and learn.

01 / FLIGHT

Rocketry & flight

Design airframes, simulate flights, and build rockets for the American Rocketry Challenge.

02 / ELECTRONICS

Avionics & electronics

Develop flight computers, custom circuit boards, sensors, and the systems that fly onboard.

03 / ENGINEERING

Design & fabrication

Use CAD, simulation, composites, and 3D printing to turn an idea into flight-ready hardware.

04 / RESEARCH

Experiments & software

Build scientific payloads, analyze flight data, and create software tools that support the team.

RESEARCH PAYLOAD

NASA TechRise Challenge

A multispectral camera and onboard sensors powered a stratospheric payload exploring machine-learning approaches to NDVI mapping.

FLIGHT CONTROL

Active airbrake system

Servo-driven airbrakes, a custom circuit board, and flight-control software designed to help a rocket reach its target apogee.

AVIONICS

Rocket++ flight computer

A custom flight computer for flight-state detection, dual-deploy recovery, and onboard telemetry logging.

COMPETITION

American Rocketry Challenge

Teams design and fly rockets to meet altitude and duration targets while carrying a raw-egg payload.
