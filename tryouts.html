2026–27 recruitment

Ready for takeoff?

Join South Brunswick Aerospace Club for a season of building, testing, launching and learning together. After winning the 2026 NASA TechRise Challenge and reaching the American Rocketry Challenge Nationals three years in a row, we’re looking for curious students ready to help shape what comes next. New this season, we are recruiting students for Genes in Space, Aviation Design Challenge, and UAV competitions in addition to the American Rocketry Challenge and the NASA TechRise Challenge.

WHO CAN JOIN

Students interested in aerospace are welcome, whether they attend South Brunswick High School or not.

South Brunswick High School students

After trying for many years, this year Zafir Shamsi and the team have been able to get the club formalized at SBHS, with the goal of having students’ efforts recognized on their transcripts.

Visit the SBHS Aerospace Club ↗

Students from other schools

If you don’t attend SBHS or you did not make it onto the SBHS team, you can still learn aerospace with our independent nonprofit. We offer outreach and can sponsor teams beyond SBHS.

Middle school and non SBHS teams

Explore aerospace with us.
