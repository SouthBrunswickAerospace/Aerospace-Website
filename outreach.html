In our community

Aerospace is better when it’s shared.

A look at two ways SBAerospace members have connected with learners and fellow rocketry teams.

South Brunswick Public Library · Grades 6–9

Introducing students to aeronautics

Zafir Shamsi and Team A led an Introduction to Aeronautics workshop for students in grades 6 through 9, sharing their interest in flight with younger learners in the community.

American Rocketry Challenge · 2026

Sharing the design process

At ARC, Zafir Shamsi shared the team’s advanced rocket design and build with students from other teams and their advisors—exchanging ideas with the wider rocketry community.
