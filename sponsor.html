Your impact

Where support takes us

Every contribution goes toward the hands-on work that turns engineering into experience.

01 / PROPULSION

Motors & flight tests

Motors run about $40 each. Competition seasons require several, including test flights.

02 / AVIONICS

Electronics & sensors

PCB fabrication, microcontrollers, sensors, and RF hardware power Rocket++ and our custom avionics.

03 / STRUCTURES

Airframe materials

Fiberglass tubing, phenolic couplers, fin stock, and recovery hardware are used in each build cycle.

04 / COMPETITION

Launches & travel

ARC registration, launch-field fees, and travel help students bring their work to the flight line.
