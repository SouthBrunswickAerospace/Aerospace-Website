Our team

One team.
Many roles.

South Brunswick Aerospace Club is one unified club with eight specialized teams and subteams—from rocketry and electronics to biology and outreach—competing in the American Rocketry Challenge, NASA TechRise, Genes In Space, MATE ROV, and more.

Each member develops a role: the skill set they build and the part of the mission they own. Roles contribute to different competitions and projects throughout the year.

One club, eight roles

Specialists work better together.

Every role contributes to the same mission, across projects and competitions.

How It Works

Your role is your starting point,
not your ceiling.

Every member holds a role that defines their core contribution. Roles overlap, inform each other, and feed into shared outcomes. A member in the Rocketry role might work on an airframe for ARC in the fall, then collaborate with Biology on a Genes In Space experiment in the spring.

Not every role touches every competition. That's intentional. Below you'll find which roles drive which projects, so you can join the role that matches what you want to build and learn.

How We Work

Roles specialize.
The team integrates.

Your role is your home base, not your limit. A fin designed by Mechanical has to fit around wires from Electronics. Biology experiments need Chemistry to validate materials. Software simulations inform Rocketry's motor selection. Nobody works in a vacuum.

We run cross-role design reviews before finalizing any build and hold weekly all-hands meetings to keep everyone aligned across competitions. Finances and Outreach make sure the work gets funded and seen.

Weekly All-Hands

Every role presents progress. Leads surface integration concerns early so nothing stalls another team's work.

Cross-role mentorship

Electronics teaches soldering. Biology teaches experiment design. Everyone teaches and everyone learns.

Shared documentation

Every design file, protocol, budget sheet, and meeting note lives in our shared GitHub org and Drive.

Find your role. Join the team.

We take new members every semester across all eight roles. No prior experience needed. Come curious and ready to work.
